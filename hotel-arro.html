EB-5 Cannot Serve as Pref Takeout Ivan Jerskey confirmed that the EB-5 capital is construction-only and is not available as a preferred equity takeout source. This eliminates the standard pref repayment mechanism and leaves the townhome sale proceeds as the primary viable repayment path. The refi horizon is 36 to 44 months for hotel stabilization — an extended hold that increases Pedison's exposure window materially.
Section 03
Preferred Equity Structure — Key Concerns
Ask Escalation — $4M to $5M to $10M The preferred equity ask has grown from an original $4M to $5M to $10M over the course of the engagement. Ivan Jerskey's stated justification was "sponsor runway." Lawrence Hill challenged this directly as vague and insufficiently supported. The use of proceeds for the incremental ask has not been clearly documented.
Townhome Sale Proceeds — Primary Repayment Mechanic With EB-5 capital unavailable as a takeout source and a 36 to 44 month hotel refi horizon, the townhome sale proceeds represent the most viable pref repayment path. First townhome sales are expected August 2026. Pedison has requested the full absorption schedule, townhome sales status, and waterfall structure as part of the outstanding diligence request.
Pedison Position Lawrence Hill issued a consolidated diligence request covering use of proceeds, townhome sales status, absorption schedule, and waterfall structure. No sponsor call and no committee review will occur until all eight diligence items are received and reviewed. The deal is on hold pending Ivan Jerskey's response.
